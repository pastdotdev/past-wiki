# Who we are

Harbor Loop sells telemetry for municipal bike-share fleets. A small box on each bike reports
position, battery and lock state every thirty seconds; our dashboard turns that into
rebalancing routes, theft alerts and monthly ridership reports for the city.

We are eight people, all remote, spread between Lisbon, Rotterdam and Montreal. There is no
office. We meet in person twice a year.
