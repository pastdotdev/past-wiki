# Decision: on-call rotation gets a fourth engineer

## What changes

Priya joins the on-call rotation from 1 June 2026. The rotation becomes Teun, Mara, Julien,
Priya, one week each, so every engineer is on call one week in four instead of one in three.
Weeks hand over on Monday at 10:00 CET, right after planning.

## Why

Three-week rotations meant each engineer spent seventeen weeks a year on call. Since the
Hetzner move the pages are rare (two in April, none in May so far) and mostly about the
rebalancing model's nightly job, which Priya owns anyway.

## Considered instead

- Hiring a contractor for nights and weekends: too expensive for the current page volume.
- Dropping on-call and relying on Yara's morning triage: cities are promised an outage
  notice within an hour, at any hour.

Signed off by Teun, 18 May 2026.
