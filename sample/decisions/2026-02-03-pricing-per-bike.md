# Decision: price per bike per month

## What changes

From 1 April 2026 every new contract is priced at 2.40 EUR per bike per month, invoiced
quarterly in advance. Existing contracts move to it at their next renewal. The old flat
fee tiers (Small, Medium, Large) are retired for new customers.

## Why

Three of four cities asked for a number they could put in a budget line that grows with
the fleet. The flat tiers made Ghent, at 1,400 bikes, our cheapest customer per bike and
Aveiro, at 210, our most expensive. Per-bike pricing also lets Ines quote a new city from
a single number.

## Considered instead

- Per ride: cities did not want to share ride counts with a vendor before signing.
- Keeping tiers with narrower bands: still lumpy at the edges, and harder to explain.

Signed off by Ines, 3 February 2026.
